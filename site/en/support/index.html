Self-check

If the connection will not come up

Four steps that solve most situations in a couple of minutes.

1

Reconnect

Turn the connection off and back on in the app.

2

Change the region

Sometimes another country or Best Server mode helps.

3

Check the network

Does any site open without the VPN? If not, the problem is your internet connection itself.

4

Update the app

Download the latest version from the downloads page and start it again.

Still stuck? Write to us with your device model, Android version and the region you picked — that is enough to start.
