FAQ

FAQ

What is GlukVPN?
GlukVPN is a VPN app with one screen and one button. It encrypts traffic to the region you pick and shows a clear connection status: region, latency and session time.
Which regions are available now?
Germany, France and the US are live now. Other regions are marked “Soon” on the map and will appear only once they actually work.
Is there a free plan?
Yes. The Free plan gives one region and one device at limited speed. No card needed to start.
Which devices does the app run on?
The Android version is available now. Windows, iOS and macOS builds are in development — we do not publish links to things that do not exist yet.
Do you keep logs of visited sites?
No. We do not store browsing history or traffic contents. Accounts and limits do need some technical data — exactly which is described in the privacy policy.
What is a kill switch?
It guards against accidental leaks: if the tunnel drops, the app blocks traffic until the connection is restored instead of letting it out directly.
How do I pay for a paid plan?
Prices on the site are preliminary. In-app payment arrives together with billing; until then you can use the Free plan.
