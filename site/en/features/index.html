Everything it does GlukVPN

Short and free of marketing noise: what works today, how it looks in the app and what a VPN fundamentally does not do.

Private by default

We do not collect browsing history and we do not sell data. An account needs a minimum of technical data, all of it listed in the policy.

Fast connection

A light client and a modern protocol: the tunnel comes up in seconds and does not drain the battery in the background.

Regions to choose from

Germany, France and the US are available now. New locations show up on the list only once they actually work.

Best Server

Do not feel like choosing manually? The app picks the region with the best latency and the lowest load.

Kill switch

If the tunnel drops, traffic is blocked instead of leaking to the open network. Once the link is back, the connection returns automatically.

DNS inside the tunnel

Name lookups never leak to your ISP or to the owner of a public hotspot.

Device management

Your devices and active sessions are listed right in the app — extra ones disconnect in one tap.

Clear statuses

Colour and caption always tell you what is going on: connected, connecting or disconnected.

No ads, no trackers

The app ships without ad SDKs or behavioural analytics built for selling audiences.

What a VPN does not do

That matters just as much as the feature list.

  • It does not make you fully anonymous: if you are signed in to a site, that site knows who you are.
  • It is not an antivirus and does not protect against phishing or malicious files.
  • It does not increase your ISP speed — it only changes the traffic route.
  • It is not meant for unlawful activity — that breaks the terms of use.
