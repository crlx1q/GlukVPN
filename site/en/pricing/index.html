Payments

Plan questions

Can I use it for free?
Yes. The Free plan needs no payment and no card: one region, one device and limited speed.
When do payments go live?
Payments switch on together with billing. Until then the prices on the site are preliminary and may change.
Can I switch plans?
Yes, you can change the plan in the app at any time. Your account and device list stay in place.
How many devices can I connect?
Free covers one device, Basic up to three, Pro up to ten. The device list lives in the app and extra devices can be disconnected.

Ready to try it?

Install the app and start on the free plan — you can move to Basic or Pro later.
